Enter a control number above to see its status and history. Results require an active connection to the barangay system.

What a status means

  • Submitted

    We received your request and assigned a control number.

  • Under review

    Staff are checking your details and requirements.

  • Approved

    Your request passed review and the document is being prepared.

  • Released

    Ready for pickup. Your SMS has the pickup date.

  • Rejected

    The request was returned with a reason you can read and act on.

  • Cancelled

    The request was cancelled before it was released.
