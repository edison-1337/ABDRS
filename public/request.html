What happens next

  1. Submit the request

    You receive a control number such as BRGY-2026-000041.

  2. Staff review

    We check your details and requirements, and text you at each change.

  3. Approval and preparation

    Once approved, the document is prepared for release.

  4. Pickup

    We text you the pickup date. Bring the control number and a valid ID.

Before you submit

  • Check that your mobile number is active and correct.
  • Upload readable photos or scans of each required document.
  • Keep your control number once it is issued.
