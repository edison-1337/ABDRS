How it works

Four steps from request to pickup.

  1. 1

    Choose a document

    Browse the available documents and open the request form for the one you need.

  2. 2

    Submit your details

    Provide your contact number, a valid ID, and any other requirements the document needs.

  3. 3

    We review it

    Barangay staff check your request and requirements. You receive an SMS at each status change.

  4. 4

    Claim your document

    Once released, we text you the pickup date. Bring the control number and a valid ID.

Documents you can request

Open a document to start a request. The catalog is maintained by barangay staff, and each type lists the documents you must upload.

Barangay Clearance

Proof that you are a resident in good standing, often required for employment, permits, and other official transactions.

Certificate of Residency

Confirms your current address within the barangay.

Certificate of Indigency

Certifies limited income, commonly required for medical or financial assistance.

Barangay Business Clearance

Required to operate a business within the barangay.

First Time Job Seeker Certificate

Issued for first-time job seekers under the First Time Job Seekers Assistance Act.

Track a request

Enter the control number from your SMS to see where your request is. You do not need an account to check its status.

It looks like BRGY-2026-000041.

What a status means

  • Submitted

    We received your request and assigned a control number.

  • Under review

    Staff are checking your details and requirements.

  • Approved

    Your request passed review and the document is being prepared.

  • Released

    Ready for pickup. Your SMS has the pickup date.

Every status, explained

A request moves along one main path, and can end early as rejected or cancelled. Each change is sent to you by SMS.

Submitted

Your request is received and has a control number.

Under review

Barangay staff are checking your details and requirements.

Approved

Your request passed review. The document is being prepared.

Released

Ready for pickup. Check your SMS for the pickup date.

Rejected

Returned with a reason you can read and act on.

Cancelled

You cancelled the request before it was released.

Common questions

Do I need an account to file a request?

You need an account to file and manage a request. You do not need an account to track a request with its control number.

What is a control number?

A unique reference such as BRGY-2026-000041. Keep it to track your request and to quote at the counter. It is assigned once and never changes.

How will I know my document is ready?

We send an SMS to the mobile number on your request at each status change, including the pickup date once the request is released.

What documents do I need to prepare?

It depends on the document type. The request form lists the required documents for the type you choose, and you upload them as part of the request.

Who can see my information?

Your requests are visible only to you and to authorized barangay staff. Contact numbers and personal details are never shown publicly.

Ready to file a request?

Create an account, choose your document, and upload the requirements. We will text you as your request moves.
